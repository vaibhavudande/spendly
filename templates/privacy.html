Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide Spendly, we collect only what we need:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data: the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to record.
  • Basic technical data: information such as session cookies needed to keep you signed in.

We do not collect bank account numbers, card details, or connect to your financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to run and improve the Service, including to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store, display, and summarise your expenses.
  • Generate category breakdowns and spending reports for you.
  • Respond to support requests and send important service notices.

We do not sell your personal or financial information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information and expense records are removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted providers, such as hosting and font services, to operate the website. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their function and are not permitted to use it for any other purpose.

We will never share your expense data with third parties except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to access, correct, or delete your data, contact us at support@spendly.example.

See also our Terms and Conditions.
